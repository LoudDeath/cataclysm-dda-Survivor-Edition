Every game archive is prepared to include legal/source/corresponding-source.zip:
program sources and headers, MSVC projects, build resources and scripts, full
editable edition PO, exact source archives for all16 vcpkg runtime packages,
20 pinned ports with patches and vcpkg build recipes. SHA512 values match
installed SPDX records. General build tools and System Libraries are treated
under GPLv3 section1 exceptions. LGPL source/relink-kit.zip and the exact
Unifont12.0.01 source also accompany the game.

actual VK Play terms/delivery, resource clearance and publisher identity remain
open checks. Hash verification does not establish any of those results.
